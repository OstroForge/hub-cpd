# COPD deck: abbreviations and repeated slides

Source: the live COPD deck, 34 slides, in the order the room sees them. Audience wording is what was read. Instructor notes are marked when an abbreviation is only there.

41 abbreviations. 27 are not spelled out on a slide. 6 are only in the instructor notes. 5 places where slides could join.

COPD is not automatic Scale 2, 88–92% does not choose the scale, and a critically ill patient still gets oxygen. That line is supposed to return. The joins below are the places where a later slide adds no new picture, quote, or question.

## Where slides repeat

Slide numbers follow the deck: 1 is the title, 34 is the certificate.

| Slides | What they share | What could change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13 and 14 | Same patient, two scales — then “a usual SpO2 of 90% scoring 3” | Slide 13 already shows 90% on air as 3 on Scale 1 and 0 on Scale 2. Slide 14 repeats that and adds the handover sentence. One slide can keep the monitor, the table, and the line: NEWS 3, all from sats, usual 90% on air, Scale 1, targeting 88–92%. |
| 18, 19 and 20 | Bronchodilators — the quotes — what to do after the neb | All three are the 6-minute rule. Slide 18 has the doses and the steps. Slide 19 is the two JRCALC quotes. Slide 20 says the same thing as “what it says” and “what it does not say”. Keep the doses, put the two quotes underneath, and keep one card: there is no two-neb cap. The neb quiz still checks the 6 minutes. |
| 24 and 27 | This patient needs high-flow oxygen — then the critically ill quiz | Slides 8 and 9 already teach “do not withhold high-flow”. Slide 24 then shows the shocked patient, and the quiz shows the same monitor and asks the same question. The quiz can be the first time the room sees that patient. The one new line on slide 24 is that Scale 2 comes from the documented type 2, not from the oxygen just applied. That line can live in the quiz answer. |
| 9 against 17 and 18 | The on-scene map also starts the drugs | The “not critically ill” box already gives 28% Venturi, salbutamol 5 mg and ipratropium. Those return on the oxygen slide and the neb slide. The map can stay a yes/no decision and leave the doses to the treatment slides. |
| 3 and 4 | Learning objectives already state the conclusion | The objectives banners say COPD is not type 2 failure and not automatic Scale 2, then slide 4 says it again with the JRCALC sentence. The four “by the end” lines can stand alone. Slide 4 can be the first time that conclusion is said. |

## Leave these as they are

The icebreaker, the two scoring patients, and the five checks are the room doing the work. Slide 11 is the new evidence, the 1-in-5 figure. Slide 15 is the pre-alert worry, which is a different question from the score of 3. Slide 16 is the opposite example, where Scale 2 is the right score. Slide 6 and the last quiz are a teach-then-check pair. The five closing points are the ending, not a second copy of an earlier slide.
